When Contacting Support

A short description of the issue is usually enough to get started. If relevant, include your device type, app version, and the email address associated with your ProxyFit account. Screenshots can help diagnose visual bugs, but avoid sending sensitive information unless necessary.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account inside ProxyFit from Account Settings. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email or use the Data Deletion Page.

Health and Safety

ProxyFit is a fitness tracking and motivation tool, not medical care. Stop exercising if something feels unsafe and seek appropriate medical help when needed.
