ProxyFit · Last updated: May 23, 2026

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Service Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your ProxyFit account and associated app data.

Delete Inside the App

  1. Open ProxyFit.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Deleting your account permanently removes the account and its associated app data. This cannot be undone.

Can't Access the App?

Request Deletion by Email

Send the request from the email address connected to your ProxyFit account.

Request Deletion

Data Deleted

  • Account authentication data.
  • Workout history and custom exercise data.
  • Character, Spotter, boss, skill, reward, and game progress data.
  • Coaching-related account data where applicable.
  • Support, data-export, and deletion-request records associated with the account, except where retention is technically or legally required.

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, anti-fraud, billing, or app store obligations. Optional deletion feedback may be saved separately as anonymized product feedback.
