Guidance Built Around You.

Work with a coach through to help keep your training moving toward your goals. Here's how ProxyFit coaching works:

  • Select "Apply for Coaching" within the app.
  • Fill out the questionnaire.
  • A coach will review your application.
  • If accepted, you will be notified inside the app to proceed with payment ($50/monthly).
  • Your coach will build workouts tailored to you specifically.
  • Complete weekly check-ins
  • Your coach will monitor your progress and adjust workout plans as necessary.
  • If any questions or concerns arise, you'll be able to communicate with your coach through our chat feature.
