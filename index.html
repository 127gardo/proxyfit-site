How it works

Your workout is still the point.

ProxyFit doesn't replace training with a game. It adds another layer of progress on top of the workouts you're already doing.

Train

Complete your normal gym workout or fitness routine.

Track

Record exercises and performance so you can see what you actually did.

Progress

Your real activity helps strengthen and develop your Proxy.

Battle

Use that progress in battles, rounds, unlocks, and character progression.

The difference

A workout tracker with a reason to care about the numbers.

A lot of fitness apps stop after recording what you did. ProxyFit uses those workouts as input for a second progression system. The goal is simple: make logging and improving your real workouts feel more satisfying without getting in the way of the fitness tools.
