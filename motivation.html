Motivation

Make progress easier to notice.

Motivation isn't constant. A useful system makes it easier to see that showing up, logging workouts, and improving performance are leading somewhere.

Use smaller wins

Instead of treating fitness as one distant goal, look for repeatable wins: completing the planned session, adding a rep, improving technique, or returning for the next workout.

Make progress visible

A workout log turns vague memories into a history. ProxyFit adds another visible layer by connecting your real training to attributes and character progression.

Why the game layer exists

The goal isn't to pretend exercise is a video game. The goal is to borrow the feedback that games are good at—progress, stats, challenges, and rewards—and attach it to something useful you're doing in real life.
