Workout tracking

A straightforward gym tracker—with something extra.

ProxyFit gives you a place to record training and review progress, then connects that real workout data to character progression and battles.

What should you track?

For most strength-training exercises, the useful basics are the exercise, weight, repetitions, and sets. Over time, those records help you compare sessions instead of relying on memory.

Why tracking matters

Progress is easier to recognize when you can look back and see that you're lifting more weight, completing more reps, or training more consistently. ProxyFit keeps the fitness side useful on its own rather than requiring you to care about the game layer.

Then ProxyFit adds a second feedback loop

Your real training helps develop your Proxy and its attributes. That means the numbers you record don't only sit in a workout history—they contribute to another form of progression you can see and use.

ProxyFit's approach: fitness app first, game second. You can focus on tracking workouts while treating battles and character progression as an optional motivation layer.
