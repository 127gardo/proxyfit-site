A Gym Tracker with Something Extra.

ProxyFit gives you a place to record your training and review your progress, then connects that real workout data to character progression. Results from the gym are not visible on our bodies immediately. The purpose of the character is to show that you are making progress even though you may not see that in real life.

What Should You Track?

For most strength training exercises, the useful basics are the exercise, weight, repetitions, and sets. Over time, those records help you compare sessions instead of relying on memory. You have the option to add more tracking options, such as laps, intensity, time, and more.

Why Tracking Matters

Progress is easier to recognize when you can look back and see what you were doing before compared to what you're doing in the future. That could be a variety of things such as completing more reps, or training more consistently.

Then ProxyFit Adds a Second Feedback Loop

Your real training helps develop your in-game character. That means the numbers you record don't just sit in your workout history, they contribute to another form of progression you can see and use.

ProxyFit's approach: Fitness app first, game second. You can focus on tracking workouts while treating battles and character progression as an optional motivation layer.
