Fitness for gamers

Real workouts. Familiar progression.

If stats, characters, and progression make games satisfying, ProxyFit applies some of those ideas to the workouts you're actually doing.

Your character doesn't replace your workout

You still train in the real world and log real exercises. ProxyFit then uses that activity as part of its progression system.

Why that can feel different

Traditional trackers are useful, but a completed workout often ends with a list of numbers. ProxyFit gives those numbers a second purpose by connecting them to your Proxy, attributes, battles, and unlocks.

You don't need to be a hardcore gamer

The game system is there as an additional incentive. The core idea remains simple enough for someone who just wants to record workouts and see progress.
