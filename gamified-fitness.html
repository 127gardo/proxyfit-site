Gamified fitness

What does “gamified fitness” mean?

Gamified fitness adds game-like feedback—such as stats, progression, goals, challenges, or rewards—to real physical activity.

It shouldn't replace the useful part

A game layer only helps if the underlying fitness tool still works for the person using it. ProxyFit is designed as a workout tracker first, with progression built around the activity you log.

How ProxyFit uses it

When you train and record workouts, that progress connects to your Proxy and attributes such as Strength, Endurance, and Speed. You can then use the character you've developed in battles and increasingly difficult rounds.

Who is it for?

It can appeal to someone who already enjoys games, but also to anyone who finds ordinary fitness tracking useful yet wants a more engaging sense of progression.
