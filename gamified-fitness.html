What Does “Gamified Fitness” Mean?

Gamified Fitness adds game-like feedback such as stats, progression, goals, challenges, and rewards for your real physical activity.

It Shouldn't Replace What Already Works

A game layer only helps if the underlying fitness tool still works for the person using it. ProxyFit is designed as a workout tracker first, with progression built around the activity you log.

How ProxyFit Uses It

When you train and record workouts, that activity contributes to your in game character. For each workout, you gain points to upgrade your attributes such as Strength, Endurance, and Speed. You can then take the character you've developed into battles and fight increasingly difficult bosses.

Who Is It For?

It can appeal to someone who already enjoys games, but also to anyone who finds ordinary fitness tracking useful yet wants a more engaging sense of progression. If games are not for you, that's fine! It is a completely optional part of the app.
