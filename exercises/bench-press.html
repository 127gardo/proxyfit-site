Bench Press

Build your chest and pressing strength with a stable setup and a controlled press.

Bench and bar17-second video

Two points to remember

Form reference: ACE Exercise Library

▶ Watch the demonstration

Bench Press video demonstration

Target muscles

Chest (pectorals)

Also works: Triceps and front shoulders

How to do it

  1. Lie on a flat bench. Keep your feet firmly on the floor and your head, upper back, and hips supported. A small, comfortable lower-back arch is enough.
  2. Grip the bar a little wider than your shoulders, with your thumbs wrapped around it. Unrack carefully and keep your wrists stacked over your forearms.
  3. Lower the bar slowly toward your mid-to-lower chest. Let your elbows angle slightly toward your sides rather than straight out.
  4. Press the bar up until your arms are straight. Keep your hips on the bench and repeat with control.

Set your feet. Stay steady. Press with control.

Use a spotter or correctly positioned safety supports when pressing a bar.

Common mistakes

  • Bouncing the bar off your chest or rushing the lowering phase.
  • Flaring your elbows straight out or letting your wrists bend far back.
  • Lifting your hips or forcing an exaggerated lower-back arch.
